About the program

The Nithub Accelerator is built for digital startups across Africa looking to move from early traction to scale. Over four months, selected founders will
gain access to tailored growth resources, mentorship, and strategic ecosystem support-including technical advisory and tools worth thousands of dollars.

What you'll get

Comprehensive Support for Your Startup Journey

Our program is designed to provide you with the resources and guidance you need to thrive.

Who Should Apply

  • Early-stage startups (with MVP and initial traction)
  • Digital solutions only (web, mobile, SaaS, etc.)
  • At least one technical co-founder
  • Operating in or targeting the African market
